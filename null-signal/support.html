Null Signal: Support

Null Signal is a single-player puzzle game for iPhone and iPad by Latchwork Studio.

Contact

Email latchworkstudio@gmail.com. Please include your device model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. We aim to reply within a few days.

Common questions

I'm stuck on a puzzle.
Tap Hint. Each puzzle has three hint steps; the third is the answer.

Does the game save automatically?
Yes, each time you solve a puzzle. You can also tap Save in the Menu at any time. Continue on the title screen returns you to where you were.

How do I start over?
Choose New Game on the title screen. This replaces your saved progress.

I can't hear the voice or the music.
Check the ring/silent setting and volume on your device, then open Sound from the title screen or the in-game Menu and raise the Music, Voice and Effects sliders.

What is free and what is paid?
Act 1 is free. One purchase unlocks Acts 2, 3 and 4 and both endings. There are no ads and nothing else to buy.

I bought the full game but it is locked (new device or reinstall).
On the title screen tap Unlock Full Game, then Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Which devices are supported?
iPhone and iPad on iOS 17 or later. The game plays in landscape.

Does the game need an internet connection?
No.

Refunds
Refunds are handled by Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy
