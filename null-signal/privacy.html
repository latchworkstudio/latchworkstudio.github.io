Null Signal: Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Null Signal is a single-player puzzle game for iPhone and iPad, published by Latchwork Studio.

What we collect

Nothing. Null Signal does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information.

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.
  • The game works fully offline.

What stays on your device

Your progress (solved puzzles, logs you have read, sound settings) is saved in the app's own storage on your device. It is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers that information.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
